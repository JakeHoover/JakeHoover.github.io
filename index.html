About

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences at the University of Alberta in Edmonton, where I also completed my BSc and MSc.

My research explores Bayesian networks, time series, functional data analysis, and model-based clustering, with a focus on developing statistical methodology for complex dependent data. I am currently placing an emphasis on hidden semi-Markov models and their extensions to more complicated data settings in order to better analyze polysomnography data sets.

I work as a research assistant fitting hidden semi-Markov models to sleep (polysomnography) data, and as a teaching assistant and statistical consultant with the university's Training and Consulting Centre. I also tutor university and high school students in statistics and mathematics.

My poster on sieve-estimated functional autoregressive HMMs won Best PhD Poster at the 2026 Meeting of Alberta Statisticians, and I was named Student of the Month in March 2026.

Research & Projects

  • General dwell time hidden semi-Markov models

    Is it worth the extra complexity of choosing a sojourn distribution for each state? A simulation study comparing HSMMs using optimal transport (Wasserstein) distances.

  • Sieve-estimated functional autoregressive HMM

    A hidden Markov model for functional time series whose regimes differ in autoregressive persistence rather than level.

  • HSMMs for sleep data

    Fitting hidden semi-Markov models to polysomnography data sets, with an interdisciplinary team of sleep experts.
